# 4. UNDERSTAND THE PRODUCT BEFORE SELLING IT

It enables real people, experts, entrepreneurs, professionals, creators and mentors to create AI identities representing their knowledge, experiences, perspectives and expertise.

Its differentiation is the human expertise, experiences, perspectives and knowledge behind individual AI identities.

# 5. THE MOST IMPORTANT STRATEGIC DECISION: SIMPLIFY THE MESSAGE

The current product concept contains many capabilities.

Do not present all of them immediately.

Do not make visitors learn the entire platform before understanding why it matters.

The website must follow a progressive disclosure strategy.

### The visitor's first impression

Within approximately five seconds, visitors should understand:

- Virtual Human lets them interact with AI identities representing real people's knowledge and expertise.
- They can discover people they want to learn from.
- They can start meaningful conversations.

That is enough for the first impression.

### What to communicate later

Only after visitors understand the core value should the website introduce:

- Creating AI identities.
- Uploading expert knowledge.
- Personalized conversations.
- Persistent memory.
- Long-term relationships.
- Challenges and tasks.
- Progress tracking.
- Expert monetization.
- Other advanced capabilities.

These are supporting capabilities, not the primary headline.

### Cognitive load rules

Apply these rules throughout the website:

1. One section should communicate one main idea.
2. One headline should express one central benefit.
3. Avoid paragraphs longer than necessary.
4. Avoid unnecessary technical terminology.
5. Do not repeat the same product explanation in multiple sections.
6. Avoid presenting too many features simultaneously.
7. Use concise headings and meaningful supporting sentences.
8. Use visuals to explain what words cannot explain efficiently.
9. Avoid excessive badges, labels and decorative text.
10. Give visitors a clear next step at every important stage.

Prefer short, powerful copy over lengthy product descriptions.

Do not confuse visitors with internal product architecture, implementation details or future roadmap features.

# 8. CONVERSION PSYCHOLOGY

Use ethical conversion principles throughout the website.

### Clarity

Visitors should never have to guess what Virtual Human does.

### Curiosity

Use messaging and visuals that make visitors want to discover what an AI identity can offer.

### Relevance

Use familiar situations that visitors can identify with.

### Trust

Clearly communicate that AI identities represent real people's knowledge and experiences.

Never imply that an AI identity is the actual human being.

### Reduced friction

Keep navigation simple and CTA labels understandable.

Do not ask visitors to make unnecessary decisions before they can explore the product.

### Value before features

Explain why a capability matters before introducing how it works.

### Consistent calls to action

Use a small number of consistent CTA labels.

Primary CTA: Get Started or Explore Virtual Human.

Secondary CTA: Create Your AI Identity.

Do not introduce a different CTA label in every section.

### No manipulation

Do not use fake urgency, fake user counts, false testimonials, fabricated reviews or misleading scarcity.

Trust is a conversion asset.
