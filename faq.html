Can I create a personal AI companion as a father, mother, or spouse?

Yes, absolutely. While Virtual Human allows you to interact with expert minds, you can also create completely custom AI companions for personal relationships. Whether you want a steady father figure offering wisdom and reassurance, a nurturing mother figure providing warmth and unconditional comfort, a devoted spouse or partner sharing intimate daily reflections, or a late-night confidant, our Character Creation Studio lets you define their relationship, shared backstory, voice tone, and proactive check-in routine.

General & Technical Questions.

What is Virtual Human?

Virtual Human is an interactive AI identity and companion platform developed and operated by UnivasOS LTD. It transforms real human knowledge, experiences, opinions, frameworks and ways of thinking into conversational companions. Instead of passively consuming articles, videos or podcasts, you can engage in direct, two-way dialogue with an AI representation of an experienced person.

How are Virtual Human AI identities different from ordinary chatbots?

Generic chatbots answer questions from broad internet data without a consistent voice, personal experience or grounded viewpoint. Virtual Human identities are anchored in the specific knowledge, proven mental models, lessons and communication styles of actual individuals and mentors.

How does the AI Virtual Companion area work?

The AI Virtual Companion area provides persistent, 1-on-1 thinking partners tailored to your ongoing professional and personal growth. Your companion maintains relational memory across sessions, understands your evolving goals, assigns actionable reflection exercises, and sends personalized follow-ups tailored specifically to what you discussed.

Does Virtual Human remember previous conversations?

Yes. The platform is designed with conversational continuity, allowing AI identities and companions to remember your context, goals, challenges and progress across sessions for deeper, personalized mentorship.

How do personalized proactive check-ins work?

Rather than sending impersonal automated newsletters, your companion synthesizes key action items and dilemmas from your latest discussions and reaches out via email or in-app notifications with targeted questions, frameworks, or accountability reminders matching your exact timeline.

Who can create an AI identity?

Entrepreneurs, founders, executives, creators, researchers, specialized professionals and educators with valuable expertise and frameworks can build an AI identity on Virtual Human to make their knowledge accessible at scale.

How does an AI identity learn from an expert's knowledge?

Knowledge owners provide written documents, frameworks, case studies, essays, methodologies and guidelines. The platform organizes this material so the AI identity reflects the expert's authentic insights, reasoning patterns and boundaries while maintaining transparency.

Can I discover and interact with different AI identities?

Yes. Learners and professionals can explore a curated directory of AI identities across disciplines such as entrepreneurship, engineering leadership, product management, marketing, finance and personal development.

Is Virtual Human only for entrepreneurs?

Entrepreneurs and founders are our initial focus because access to experienced mentorship is a frequent challenge in startups. However, the platform is designed for anyone seeking wisdom across technology, creative arts, executive leadership, career growth and personal development.

Can experts monetize their AI identities?

In the platform's planned creator ecosystem, experts will have tools to offer tiered access, subscription conversations and specialized guidance sessions, allowing knowledge owners to earn sustainable returns on their curated expertise.

Can an AI identity replace speaking to the actual expert?

No. An AI identity does not replace human relationship or live consulting. It provides continuous, 24/7 access to an expert's documented thinking, helping you solve immediate questions and prepare for higher-level interactions without consuming their limited calendar time.

Is user or creator data used to train open LLM models?

Never. Your conversation transcripts and uploaded creator files are strictly siloed in private vector namespaces. They are never sold, exposed, or used to fine-tune open commercial foundational models.

How do I get started?

You can join as a learner to explore available AI identities and start your first conversation immediately, or apply as a knowledge owner to begin crafting your own interactive AI identity in our creator studio.
