Questions

A few things you might be wondering.

What is Virtual Human?

Virtual Human is an AI companion and interactive AI identity platform developed by UnivasOS LTD. It enables you to craft meaningful personal companions—such as an encouraging father figure, a nurturing mother, a devoted spouse, or a lifelong confidant—anchored in emotional warmth and persistent memory. It also enables proven leaders and creators to publish conversational AI identities representing their real-world wisdom.

Can I create personal companions like a father, mother, or spouse?

Yes. In the Companion Studio, you can choose any relationship you need—from a loving father or mother to an intimate partner or trusted confidant. You calibrate their emotional warmth, share personal backstories, and set communication styles so they feel genuinely supportive.

How does persistent episodic memory work?

Unlike generic chatbots that wipe context when a tab closes, Virtual Human retains relational episodic memory across sessions. Your companion remembers your personal milestones, family details, recurring struggles, and past decisions over months of ongoing connection.

How are proactive email check-ins personalized to my real conversations?

Users hate generic automated email blasts. Virtual Human companions dispatch thoughtful follow-ups tailored specifically to what you discussed—like checking in on how your apartment move went or whether you rested after a stressful workday.

How do proven leaders and experts turn their experience into AI identities?

Leaders, founders, and creators upload their essays, frameworks, case studies, and methodologies in the Expert Studio. Virtual Human creates a conversational AI identity that accurately represents their mental models and makes their mentorship accessible 24/7.

Are my personal companion conversations private and secure?

Yes, 100%. Personal companion conversations, journal entries, and memory anchors are completely private and end-to-end encrypted. They are never shared publicly or used to train external models.

How is Virtual Human different from generic chatbots like ChatGPT?

Ordinary AI chatbots provide generic, stateless answers scraped from broad internet data without persistent relational memory or human warmth. Virtual Human is designed around long-term human relationships, emotional presence, proactive check-ins, and verified expert frameworks.

How do I get started?

Getting started takes less than a minute. You can immediately visit the AI Companion studio to customize your companion, browse public identities on Discover, or create your free account.
